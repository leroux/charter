## STK-261126-78RN A title gives the same slug from the service and from the tool

Status: active

A writer who slugs a title through the service and through the tool gets
the same slug from both.

Why: a slug is a link; two that differ for one title are a broken link the
writer never sees until a reader follows it.

Scenarios:
1. `same-slug-both-ways` (owner, backlog 003): a title with capitals,
   spaces and punctuation gives one slug from the service and the tool.
